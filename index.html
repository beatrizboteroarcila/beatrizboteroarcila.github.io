01 About

I'm an Assistant Professor of Law at Sciences Po Paris and a Faculty Associate at Harvard's Berkman Klein Center for Internet & Society.

My work is on AI liability, data governance, and the legal and policy enablers of the digital transformation, spanning the European Union, the United States, and Latin America.

Data governance & privacy · AI liability & regulation · AI diffusion & development · EU digital regulation · Smart cities & urban tech · Platform & internet governance · Latin America tech policy
